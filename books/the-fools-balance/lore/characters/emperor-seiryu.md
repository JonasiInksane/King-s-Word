# Emperor Seiryu

Age

49

Appearance

Long silver hair.

Royal black robes.

Calm expression.

Never raises his voice.

---

Personality

Extremely intelligent.

Never emotional.

Believes sacrificing thousands to save millions is justice.

---

Power

Dragon Rei.

Domain

Imperial Decree

Reality bends to commands spoken inside his Domain.

---

Belief

Freedom creates war.

Control creates peace.

The Fool threatens everything he built.

---

Character Arc

Starts as villain.

Ends as tragic opponent.

Readers should respect him even when opposing him.
