# Inquisitor Kuro

Role

Leader of the Holy Inquisition.

---

Appearance

White robes.

Black gloves.

Eyes permanently covered.

---

Personality

Absolute conviction.

No humor.

Believes doubt itself is evil.

---

Power

Judgment Rei.

Can force confessions.

Suppresses magic.

---

Goal

Erase Chaos.

Destroy The Fool.

Protect humanity.

---

Character Arc

Starts believing

The Fool is evil.

Ends realizing

The Fool may have been the only honest person alive.
