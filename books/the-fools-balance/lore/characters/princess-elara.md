# Princess Elara Aurelius

## Role

The Future.

The bridge between Order and Chaos.

---

# Background

Daughter of King Caelum.

Raised inside perfection.

---

# Personality

- compassionate
- intelligent
- curious
- empathetic

---

# Belief

"People deserve the right to choose their own mistakes."

---

# Conflict

She loves her father.

She understands his reasons.

But she sees the cost.

---

# Character Arc

She begins believing:

The kingdom is perfect.

She ends believing:

Perfect things cannot grow.

---

# Relationship With The Fool

"What if you're wrong?"

The Fool has no answer.
