# King Caelum Aurelius

## Role

The Order.

The final ideological opponent.

---

# Background

The greatest king Aurelia has ever known.

During his reign:

- wars ended
- poverty disappeared
- crime vanished

---

# Public Image

A perfect ruler.

A father of peace.

---

# Secret

The peace required sacrifices.

Freedom.

Choice.

Individuality.

---

# Philosophy

"People do not need freedom.

They need happiness."

---

# Personality

- calm
- intelligent
- compassionate
- terrifyingly rational

He is not evil.

He genuinely believes he saved humanity.

---

# Relationship With The Fool

They are opposites.

The King believes:

Humanity must be controlled to survive.

The Fool believes:

Humanity must suffer to grow.
