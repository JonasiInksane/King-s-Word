# Sir Aldren Valemont

## Role

The Hero.

The character closest to a traditional protagonist.

---

# Background

A legendary knight of Aurelia.

Raised to believe:

A good person follows the law.

A good knight protects the innocent.

---

# Appearance

Early 30s.

Armored.

Disciplined.

Always carries the royal sword.

---

# Personality

Surface:

- honorable
- brave
- disciplined
- reliable

Hidden:

- afraid of failure
- afraid of making the wrong choice
- desperate to believe the world is simple

---

# Belief

"Without rules, people become monsters."

---

# Conflict

The Fool slowly forces Aldren to question:

Are rules protecting people?

Or are people protecting rules?

---

# Character Arc

Beginning:

The perfect knight.

Middle:

A man questioning everything.

End:

Someone who chooses his own morality.

---

# Relationship With The Fool

Starts:

"I will stop you."

Ends:

"I finally understand why you had to exist."
