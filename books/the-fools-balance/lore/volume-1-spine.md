## One-sentence spine

A mysterious clown is captured after a ruined town. While the Empire argues over what he is, the same impossible agreement begins spreading again. The people who feared him must release him to stop it—and slowly discover that the power capable of breaking the Chorus may be more horrifying than the Chorus itself.
