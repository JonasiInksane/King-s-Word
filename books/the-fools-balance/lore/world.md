# World

The world is called

## Akaritsu (暁律)

"The Law of Dawn."

No one remembers why.

---

# Continents

## Kōkoku

Land of kingdoms.

Politics.

Knights.

Academies.

Most of the story begins here.

---

## Shinkai

Eastern continent.

Old clans.

Sword schools.

Forgotten gods.

---

## Vespera

Ruined western continent.

Ancient civilization.

Forbidden technology.

Most mysteries originate here.

---

## Noctis

Northern frozen continent.

Almost unexplored.

Ancient monsters still survive.

---

## The Hollow Sea

An ocean where ships disappear.

Time behaves strangely.

Even maps refuse to stay accurate.

---

# Nations

## Akatsuki Kingdom

Primary setting.

Capital of heroes.

Royal Academy.

---

## The Holy Dominion

Controls religion.

Produces Saints.

Operates the Inquisition.

---

## Obsidian Republic

Merchants.

Scientists.

Assassins.

Money controls everything.

---

## Crimson Shogunate

Eastern empire.

Swordmasters.

Honor above all else.

---

## Ashlands

No government.

Only survivors.

Many future allies originate here.

---

# Technology

Late medieval.

Limited alchemy.

Airships exist but are rare.

Magic is more common than machinery.

---

# Monsters

Called Yokai.

Not naturally evil.

Simply distorted Rei.

Many become enemies.

Some become wiser than humans.

---

# Ancient History

Three thousand years ago...

something rewrote reality.

Nobody remembers what.

History before that point barely exists.

The truth becomes one of the story's greatest mysteries.
