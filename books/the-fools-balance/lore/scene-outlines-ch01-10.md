# Scene Outlines Chapters 1-10

## Chapter 1
# The Fool Arrives

POV:

Sir Renji Arata

---

Scene 1

Renji investigates reports of a strange criminal.

Rumors:

A thief.

A clown.

A demon.

---

Scene 2

He finds The Fool sitting in a destroyed village.

The Fool is laughing.

Renji assumes madness.

---

Scene 3

The Fool saves survivors.

Then destroys the village gate.

Renji cannot understand why.

---

Ending:

The Fool looks at Renji.

"You're a hero, right?"

"Good.

I was worried nobody would come."
