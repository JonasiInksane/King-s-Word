# Akatsuki Empire

## Overview

The Akatsuki Empire is the largest human civilization in the world.

For centuries, humanity survived through endless wars against monsters, demons, rogue magic users, and supernatural disasters.

The Empire's answer was simple:

Order.

Every person has a place.

Every ability has a classification.

Every threat has a rank.

Every hero has a purpose.

---

# Philosophy

"Chaos is the enemy of civilization."

The Empire believes humanity does not suffer because people are evil.

Humanity suffers because people are unpredictable.

Therefore:

- Magic must be registered.
- Heroes must be ranked.
- Villains must be classified.
- Dangerous individuals must be monitored.
- Unknown powers must be controlled.

---

# Government

## Emperor Seiryu

The absolute ruler.

His authority is considered necessary because only one person can make decisions without being influenced by personal emotions.

---

# Imperial Structure

## The Imperial Council

Controls:

- Economy
- Military
- Magic research
- Hero organizations
- Foreign affairs

---

## Hero System

The Empire created official hero rankings.

Ranks:

E-Rank:
Minor ability users.

D-Rank:
Local protectors.

C-Rank:
Professional heroes.

B-Rank:
Elite fighters.

A-Rank:
National defenders.

S-Rank:
World-class heroes.

SSS-Rank:
Existences beyond normal classification.

Only one person currently has an SSS classification.

Harlequin Akihiko.

---

# Military

The Imperial Army protects the borders.

Main divisions:

- Imperial Knights
- Magic Corps
- Dragon Guard
- Special Execution Units

---

# Relationship With The Fool

The Empire officially classifies Akihiko as:

Threat Level: SSS

Reason:

"An uncontrollable existence capable of destabilizing civilization."

However, the deeper truth:

Akihiko is dangerous because he cannot be categorized.

The Empire does not fear his strength.

It fears his freedom.
