# Holy Inquisition

## Overview

The Holy Inquisition exists to eliminate supernatural threats.

Originally created to protect humanity.

Eventually became the greatest organization enforcing order.

---

# Motto

"That which cannot be understood cannot be allowed to exist."

---

# Purpose

The Inquisition investigates:

- Forbidden magic.
- Demonic contracts.
- Ancient artifacts.
- Unregistered abilities.
- Reality-breaking phenomena.

---

# Leader

## Inquisitor Kuro

The Black Saint.

The strongest member of the organization.

---

# Beliefs

The Inquisition believes chaos creates suffering.

A world without freedom is preferable to a world destroyed by freedom.

---

# Methods

The Inquisition uses:

- Magical interrogation.
- Memory examination.
- Ability suppression.
- Purification rituals.
- Execution orders.

---

# Relationship With The Empire

Officially:

Partners.

Reality:

The Inquisition believes the Empire is too weak.

They believe even the Emperor's control has limits.

---

# Relationship With The Fool

Kuro considers Akihiko the ultimate sin.

Not because he kills.

Not because he destroys.

Because he proves humanity can exist outside the system.
