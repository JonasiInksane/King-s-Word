# Seven Saints

## Overview

The Seven Saints are the strongest holy warriors of humanity.

They are living symbols of hope.

---

# Public Image

To civilians:

Heroes.

To governments:

Weapons.

To enemies:

Legends.

---

# Members

Each Saint represents a virtue.

Examples:

## Saint of Courage

The strongest warrior.

## Saint of Wisdom

Master strategist.

## Saint of Mercy

The healer.

## Saint of Judgment

The executioner.

---

# Conflict

The Saints believe they represent humanity's ideals.

Akihiko challenges them:

"Are you protecting humanity...

or protecting the image humanity created?"
