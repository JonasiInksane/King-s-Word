# Hero Academy

## Overview

The Imperial Hero Academy trains the next generation of heroes.

Every official hero begins here.

---

# Purpose

The Academy teaches:

- Combat.
- Magic control.
- Monster hunting.
- Leadership.
- Hero ethics.

---

# Philosophy

"A hero is not someone with power.

A hero is someone trusted with power."

---

# Ranking

Students are assigned ranks based on:

- Ability strength.
- Combat skill.
- Intelligence.
- Emotional stability.

---

# Important Conflict

The Academy teaches heroes that:

Heroes defeat villains.

Villains threaten society.

The Fool challenges this belief.

---

# Student Reaction To Akihiko

Most students believe:

"He is a joke."

After witnessing him:

"He is terrifying."

Eventually:

"Maybe he was the only real hero."
