# Twelve Executioners

## Overview

Elite agents of the Holy Inquisition.

Their purpose:

Remove impossible threats.

---

# Members

Each Executioner specializes in killing a specific type of enemy.

Examples:

- Demon Slayer
- Magic Eraser
- Immortal Hunter
- Curse Breaker

---

# Reputation

They are feared even by heroes.

---

# Purpose

When the Empire cannot control something...

The Executioners remove it.
