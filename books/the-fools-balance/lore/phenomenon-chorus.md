# The Consensus (Enforced Harmony)

Writer-facing. Do not dump stages as exposition. Show tells.

On-page, characters may not have a clean name yet. Writer term: **Consensus**.

Related mysteries: Mystery 010, Mystery 011.

---

## Theme

**Living is carrying things. Keep carrying them.**

The phenomenon says: Rest.  
Akihiko says: Get up.

Not because getting up is easy. Because living is effort — arguing over salt, finishing ledgers, baking, writing letters, remembering names, grieving, laughing. Ordinary acts are quiet defiance.

Laughter = another way of saying *I’m still carrying it.*

---

## What it attacks

**Effort.** Not identity. Not memory.

People stop cooking, arguing, fixing roofs, writing letters, saying goodbye — not because they forgot. Because *why bother?*

Cosmic, not clinical: **entropy of the will.** Everything moves toward less effort, less choice, less pain, less self — until nothing remains.

Do **not** write it as personal depression. It is larger than mood. It pities humanity. It thinks it is helping.

---

## One-sentence engine

**Consensus removes the burden of choosing: the affected finish each other’s sentences, close every choice with exact “We decided,” and — later — reality itself begins to obey the shared conclusion.**

## Instinct (show, don’t name)

**Convince them they no longer need to struggle.**

Whisper: stop struggling, stop choosing, stop hoping, rest. Put it down.

- **Smile** = relief (effort over). Not hospitality. Not greeting.
- **“We decided.”** = peaceful — choosing lifted. Soft. Kind. Relieved.
- **Rooms** = nothing needs doing anymore. Nothing unfinished. Nobody leaves (leaving is effort / wanting).
- Later phrase evolution: “It’s easier.” Parent: “You don’t have to cry anymore.”
- **Pity, not hate.** Genuinely believes it is mercy.

**Reduce going forward:** tea, dinner, hospitality, sweetness.  
**Keep:** smiles, relief, ease, unfinished work abandoned, “why bother.”

Escalate scale (room → household → village → kingdom). At village scale, start affecting **work** (fields weed themselves, bread never burns, buckets refill) — reality helping too much. Mystery > dump. Each destination needs one unforgettable identity (“the village where…”).

**HARD — do not solve the phenomenon.** Leave unexplained consistencies. Never fully answer why two cups, why steam, why now, why Settlement answers a request. Readers love the unanswered rule more than the explanation.

**Late series line (NOT YET):** Someone asks what it wants. Akihiko: “…For you to stop.”

## Hard signature (on-page)

1. **Sentence-sharing** — finish each other’s sentences once started.
2. **Verdict phrase** — exact **“We decided.”** (relief, not cheer)
3. **Wrong smile** — calm relief that does not match pain/fear.

Renji notices. Ayame researches. Akihiko does **not** lecture the rule.

## Akihiko on-page (HARD — identity)

**The Fool NEVER speaks in conclusions.**

Renji concludes. Ayame concludes. Akihiko observes / asks / jokes / wanders.

**Opposition:** Phenomenon = Rest. Fool = Get up. Every joke is rebellion against putting the weight down.

He:

- notices sideways; points; asks strange questions
- is occasionally wrong; accidentally terrifies
- stays funny in horror
- measures whether people are still *trying* (bake, salt, letters, names, arguments)
- seed lines like “…It asked too soon.” / later “…For you to stop.”

**Compass (hard):** Entering somewhere new, notice one human thing first — evidence of effort still happening (ink under nails, muddy boots, chalk, laundry, an argument, bread smell).

**Detours (hard):** Occasionally notice something completely unrelated that never returns (“That shelf leans.”). Not every observation is symbolism. Life is messy; so is he.

He does **not** explain the phenomenon, drive the investigation, or stack cryptic lines.

**Rule on the wall:** Akihiko never explains the world. He reveals how he sees it.
