# Style Guide

## Goal

Write a novel that feels like reading an anime rather than watching one.

Readers should visualize every scene without relying on anime tropes.

---

# Inspirations

Primary

- Lord of the Mysteries
- Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint
- Re:Zero
- Hunter x Hunter
- One Piece
- Chainsaw Man
- Frieren

(Do **not** borrow Jujutsu Kaisen terminology — especially *Domain* / Domain Expansion.)

---

# Power Terms

Masters open an **Axiom** — a binding (philosophy forced into temporary law on the marked). Not a private arena. Renji's named Axiom: **Absolute Duel** (duelists locked; cannot turn to third tasks; world stays).

**Banned:** Domain, Domain Expansion, sure-hit, barrier dome, private universe / pocket dimension feel.

---

# Narrative Style

Third Person Limited

The POV character determines what the reader knows.

Never reveal another character's thoughts unless it is their POV chapter.

---

# Prose (HARD) — Quiet Confidence

Full rule + examples: `writing/prose-rhythm.md`

Narration calmly tells what happened. It does not perform the story.

**Target middle ground** (Frieren / Dungeon Meshi lane): flowing, not poetic, not clipped.

| Extreme | Symptom | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Overwritten | Metaphor stacks, personified places, every line wants admiration | Cut |
| Underwritten | Short. Short. Short. Stage directions | Expand into flow |
| Middle | Varied rhythm + precise observation + almost no commentary | Keep |

**Rhythm:** Long → medium → short (and variants). Not all short. Not all long metaphors.

**Observation, never interpretation:**

- Do: *The bells answered together.* / *The stew was hot enough to sting her tongue. She reached for another spoonful anyway.*
- Don’t: *The meadow poured music through her ribs.* / *He was overwhelmed.* / *The forest had decided to be kind.*

Let dialogue make personality, actions make emotion, the world make wonder. Trust the scene.

---

# Tone

70% Serious

20% Comedy

10% Horror

The comedy should make the horror worse.

---

# Comedy Rules

Comedy comes from character.

Never from breaking tension randomly.

The Fool creates comedy.

The world creates drama.

**Fool quote ration:** max 1–2 bumper-sticker lines per chapter. Most of his dialogue should be flat/distracted (hunger, naps, wrong answers), not a highlight reel.

---

# Action

Every fight should tell a story.

Never write a fight that only exists to show power.

Every battle must reveal:

- Character
- Philosophy
- Plot
- Mystery

---

# Dialogue

Characters should never explain the plot.

Dialogue exists to reveal personality.

---

# Exposition

Hide exposition inside conflict.

Never stop the story to explain the world.

Readers should discover information naturally.

---

# Mysteries

Always answer one mystery before introducing two new ones.

Questions create momentum.

Answers create satisfaction.

The balance creates addiction.

---

# Endings

Every chapter should end with one of the following:

- A revelation
- A decision
- A twist
- An emotional payoff
- A mystery

Never end on filler.
