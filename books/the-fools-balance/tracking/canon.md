# World

World Name:
Akaritsu

Ancient civilization vanished approximately 3000 years ago.

Magic exists through Rei.

Heroes and Villains are recognized by society.

# Magic

Three primary disciplines (glossed on-page Ch 1):

- Shiki (body)
- Kotodama (spoken seal / word)
- Kage (perception / what the eye trusts)

Axioms exist (bindings: philosophy forced into temporary law on the marked — not private arenas / Domains). Renji’s Absolute Duel = duelists locked until yield/defeat/death; cannot turn to third tasks.

Resonance exists (named in dialogue; not fully explained).

Paper Rei (Ayame): living paper constructs / information work (Ch 3).

# Timeline (chapter facts)

Chapter 1

- Renji meets Harlequin Akihiko at ruined Ashvale.
- Akihiko saves survivors from a collapsing house in a way that looks careless and may be precise.
- Akihiko and Renji clash briefly; outcome ambiguous — Renji cannot give a single clean account.
- Akihiko removes Ashvale’s gate-as-barrier without a conventional shatter; Renji’s protective Rei finds nothing to catch; Renji briefly cannot remember hinge height.
- Akihiko was waiting for a Hero; confirms Renji’s identity with relief. Mask always worn.

Chapter 2

- Renji challenges Akihiko at Ashvale; Akihiko refuses a proper fight.
- Extended clash: Renji cannot land a clean containment; misses remain ambiguous (habit / luck / reading).
- Akihiko identifies Renji’s left-hip telegraph and Axiom intent before Renji commits.
- Renji refuses Absolute Duel with survivors present; Akihiko stumbles into the civilian concern (“Oh, wait. The children”).
- Renji’s discipline surprises Akihiko. Renji resists distraction (“Most people are easier to distract”).
- Renji concludes Akihiko knows *him* (not proven future-sight); was studying, not avoiding.
- Akihiko frames pursuit as chasing vs following; leaves toward capital road.
- Renji’s working conclusion: Akihiko had been waiting for Renji specifically (unverified motive).

Chapter 3

- Court threat assessment; Ayame assigned to gather facts.
- Fool trail records are morally muddy (retreat-road dead; collateral family; missing priests + returned children).
- False leads burned (bandits / Church / theater troupe / Renji inventing him).
- Sealed North Wall folder: *Subject recovered after cessation of hostilities. No memories. Broken white mask.* Designation scratched out.
- Ayame’s hook: Recovered from what? (Kingdom-save line not in folder.)

Chapter 4

- Fool enters Imperial Hero Academy; mocked; containment pending.
- Breach-beast attack; Academy response fails; panic.
- Fool unmakes beast in a way witnesses call luck / unclassified; Renji binds him and removes him by carriage.

Chapter 5

- Fool POV: remembers everything; joking is load-bearing.
- Soft-bound in carriage/holding; could break seals, doesn’t.
- Capital suffering seen; missing-persons list touches known names.
- Church inbound; Ayame requested statement copies.
- To Renji: “If I stop joking, I have to remember why I started.”

Chapter 6

- Kuro + Shigure arrive; jurisdiction clash with Renji/Ayame.
- Fool feeds street children in Command service court under seals.
- Judgment Rei fails to get a single clean confession (stacked guilt).
- Fool admits he could break seals; refuses missing-person names.
- Kuro postpones execution; watches Renji and Ayame; presses for North Wall scrap.

Chapter 7

- Court decree: Fool destroyed Homura (river city). Prior story (3 months): demon raid.
- Survivor Old Den: Homura under demon agreement (“nailed smiles”); Fool broke the hold; fire = hold fighting back; thousands lived.
- Demon remnant briefly re-possesses Den at refugee row; Renji cuts seam; Fool’s bell stutters possession.
- Decree still stands publicly; Kuro smells demon truth but will not scrap the monster narrative cleanly.
- Renji commits to an “ugly report” against the cleaner story.
