# Chapter Timeline

## Chapter 1 — The Fool Arrives

POV: Sir Renji Arata

- Renji investigates three conflicting reports (thief / clown / demon); margin note mentions a bell.
- Commander Hoshikawa: Identify. Contain. Do not escalate without cause.
- Ashvale ruined; Akihiko waiting, laughing; “You’re early” / bored.
- Claims absence during attack; “door everyone agreed not to look for.”
- Saves survivors (dead beam); silent doorway fear in Renji.
- Brief clash; ambiguous misses.
- Gate ceases to be a barrier (not a conventional shatter); hinge-height memory blurs.
- Hook: waiting for a Hero — “I was worried nobody would come.”

## Chapter 2 — The Man Who Lost the Fight Before It Began

POV: Sir Renji Arata (immediate continuation)

- Renji challenges / demands binding; Akihiko refuses fight (“homework” / break).
- Duel in Ashvale square; survivors present; Renji herds fight away from trough.
- Misses read as habit/luck/reading — not confirmed speed power.
- Akihiko names Renji’s left-hip telegraph; Renji almost opens Absolute Duel (vow, not arena).
- Akihiko stumbles into civilian concern (“Oh, wait. The children”) — idiot-first.
- Renji aborts Axiom by discipline; warmth behind mask arrives late (expected prideful Hero, not restraint).
- Renji wins a second beat: ignores clown bait; “Most people are easier to distract.”
- Realization: not avoiding — studying (door-lock check). Renji concludes “You know me,” not prophecy.
- Akihiko toward capital road: chasing vs following.
- Hook: Renji feels he was not waiting for a Hero — waiting for Renji.

## Chapter 3 — Shape of a Villain

POV: Princess Ayame (same capital day / court reaction while Renji still in field)

- Emergency council: Ashvale ruined; Fool toward capital; Seiryu assigns Ayame facts, Command contain, Watch wards.
- Mori / Oda push “dangerous”; Ayame separates extraordinary from evil.
- Archive: trail morally muddy — caravan turnaround → four dead on retreat; sister freed → another family ruined.
- False leads: bandits (dead); Church soft work (unproven); theater Harlequin (wrong man); Renji inventing him (dies).
- Mori mid-archive: don’t make him a saint; Seiryu note — don’t fall in love with a mystery.
- Clerk: wrote “demon” to move boots; children returned; **twelve priests missing**.
- Intentional vs accidental theories pinned; neither saint nor simple demon.
- Hook folder (North Wall aftermath): *Subject recovered… No memories. Broken white mask.* — “Recovered from what?”
- Kingdom-save addendum **not** found (deferred later arc).

## Chapter 5 — The Bell

POV: Harlequin Akihiko (first rare Fool camera); carriage → Command holding

- Soft Kotodama seals; he could break them, doesn’t.
- Interior: he remembers everything (streets, Ashvale, Academy, older ceilings) — jokes so he doesn’t sit in the memory-room.

- Hook line (earned): “If I stop joking, I have to remember why I started.”
- Renji stays in the room.

## Chapter 6 — The First Inquisitor

POV: Inquisitor Kuro (with Executioner Shigure)

- Arrives capital Hero Command; Church body after Ch 3 letter.
- Jurisdiction clash: Renji (Command custody) + Ayame (no execute without Emperor mark) vs Kuro/Shigure.
- Expects monster; finds Fool in service court feeding street children (Renji allowed air under seals).
- Judgment Rei: Fool’s guilt is stacked/many-doored; returns *which crime first?*
- Wrong read: Kuro calls feeding “theater”; children/Ayame contradict.
- Interrogation: worst deed = joke used where oath should have been; refuses names; admits he could break seals, doesn’t.
- Near-cut: Shigure draws; Renji half-draws; Kuro holds.
- Decision: postpones execution (category error); Church keeps the question; Command keeps the body.
- Presses Ayame for North Wall scrap — “Recovered is a Church word.”
- Hook (to Shigure): if he is kind, kindness is no longer proof of innocence.
