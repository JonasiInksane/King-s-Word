# Canon hierarchy

1. Approved finished chapters (see `chapter-log.md`)  
2. Scene / chapter outline (`lore/plot-outline.md`)  
3. Character sheets  
4. Lore documents  
5. Tracking notes  
6. Brainstorms  

**Never canon from brainstorm alone:** dungeon creator identity, architect morality, dungeon consciousness, why creation began, what *Don’t go east* fully means until written.

**Cross-book:** Other novels in `books/` are not canon here. See `anti-leakage.md`.
