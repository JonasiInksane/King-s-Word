# Floors and routes

## Design law

Not MMO progression. Biomes vary wildly in size. Multiple routes. Maps incomplete. One floor may be a volume.

## Volume 1 — Mirror Veins

Primary biome: [`ecology/mirror-veins.md`](ecology/mirror-veins.md).

Notable zones (same “floor,” different days of travel):

| Zone | Size feel | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Ink Gate Spur | Small | Entry from Ashveil; map stalls |
| Crystal Galleries | Large | Whistle taboo; aurora beauty |
| Mirror River Road | Medium | Mana vs water; ferry culture |
| Hanging Markets | Village | Trade, philosophies clash |
| Vein Heart | Landmark | Upward waterfall (impossible) |
| Silent Gallery | Trap corridor | Terror = silence before Stalker |
| Seal Niche (discovery) | One chamber | Party accidentally frees the Jester |

## Routes (incomplete on purpose)

- **Scholar’s Trace** — slow, documented, often outdated
- **Salt Cut** — Harvester path; overhunted edges
- **Pilgrim Coil** — longer; respects Weaver bridges
- **Red Scar** — Conqueror burn-road; collapses after rains

Maps disagree because crystal growth and water-cuts moved seams — and because sellers forge certainty.
