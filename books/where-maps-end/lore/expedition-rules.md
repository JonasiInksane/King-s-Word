# Expedition Rules

Practical sayings — ecological truths, like “don’t swim where crocodiles breed.” They fail when ecosystems **evolve**, not because someone lied.

The Jester often remembers old versions. When he is wrong, the dungeon changed.

## Common upper-depth sayings

- Don’t whistle in stone throats.
- Always carry light past the first seam.
- Never eat before sunrise on a dark floor (local “sunrise” = light-cycle).
- Don’t camp near blue moss.
- Never cross the same running water twice in one march (Mirror Veins variant).
- Leave nothing behind — the dungeon remembers.
- If the forest goes silent, you are already late.

## Volume 1 (Greenwood Basin)

- If the grove goes silent, leave.
- Don’t camp on blue-glow moss.
- Don’t follow parting trees hoping for a shortcut.
- Hang your map on the Wall — or don’t expect a funeral song.
- Don’t go east (Map Wall saying; unexplained).
- Maps that agree too neatly are suspicious.

When a rule fails on-page, log it in `tracking/expedition-rule-failures.md` and ask: what evolved?
