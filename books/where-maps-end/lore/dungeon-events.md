# Dungeon Events

Nature changes. So does the dungeon. Recurring world events reshape routes, comedy, and terror.

| Event | Cadence | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Crystal Bloom** | Every ~13 years | Caves become crystal forests; routes vanish or rewrite; mana refraction spikes |
| **Great Migration** | Irregular, worsening | Predators move upward; guilds halt deep expeditions; surface bets spike |
| **Flood Season** | Seasonal in wet biomes | Floors inaccessible; maps of “dry roads” become graves |
| **Sleeping Season** | Regional / species-linked | Ancient predators go harmless; false safety kills the careless |
