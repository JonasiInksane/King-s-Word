## Volume 1 — Where the Maps Disagree

**Expedition:** The First Expedition  
**Setting:** Greenwood Basin  
**Hub:** Mossgate (Map Wall)  
**Feel:** Starts like a normal dungeon. Then none of it makes sense.  
**Length:** **21 chapters** — Chapter 21 is the volume end.

### Goal

Cartographers’ Guild: three official dated maps of the same locus disagree (1712 river / 1839 forest / 1987 grassland). Independent survey teams hired. Niko joins with Marta, Lumi, Bram.

### Answer shape (not a closed cage)

Geography used to move. Root-creatures reshaped valleys. Each map was correct in its year. Root-creatures are assumed extinct; Niko **notices** evidence they aren’t — he does not prove. End: “I don't think they're extinct.” / “Then where are they?”

### Combat pulse (Vol 1)

Leisure + adventuring: wonder and camp still carry the book, but the road is not passive. Intermittent scrapes land between wonder beats — glass wolves, beetle swarms, scavengers, bad trail encounters — short visual fights the party wins or drives off. Ecology before loot; no Demon King ladder. Apex (Hollow Stag) and basin cascade remain survive-by-reading; they are not the volume’s only action.

### Climax (Chapter 21)

**One primary save — Mossgate / the basin cascade** (Stag leaving → wolves on roads → moss bleach → beetle bloom). Redirect / stabilize — not kill the Hollow Stag.

**Not a Jester-rescue climax.** He is (writer-only) the creator / Architect of Ecology — more powerful than the party knows. There is no plot reason for them to “save” him; he does not need saving. He stays ridiculous on the surface (bells, bad clothes, jokes) while dropping **offhand observations** — especially near Lumi — that let others improve spells (“chemistry,” less waste; ice-grows-inwards style; see `lore/magic-system.md` and `writing/jester-voice.md`). Not teaching / not lecturing. Party never learns Architect truth in Vol 1 (Vol 5). Never say “First Conservatory.”

Final button still available at volume close: flower → “I've never seen this one before.” → touch → quiet laugh → **“Good.”** → “Let's go.”

### Jester

Mid-late volume (Glass Garden ~Ch 12–13). Debate. Wake. First line: “You're all terribly dressed.”  
Cover: washed-out hire, bells, bad timing. Weird only. No history confession.  
Through Ch 14–21: offhand observations / weird asides — party learns from throwaway lines; Lumi’s recipes get sharper; he may wander off or say ignore him; nobody labels it teaching.

### Left behind

Distant lights beyond the upside-down waterfall. Seen. Not entered. (Planted Ch 11; still open at Ch 21.)

**Second, smaller leave-behind (new, Ch 19):** a sealed door at the far wall of the Understars (see below) — colder and newer-feeling than Jester's own seal, roots grown the wrong way into its frame instead of out of it. Glimpsed only. Not entered. Jester goes quiet, doesn't joke. No name, no villain, no explanation on-page — see `lore/ecology/glass-garden-rooms.md` (Low Gate) and Volume 2+ seeds below. And simply: they don't have lanterns enough to see where the Understars' cavern actually ends.

### Wonder triad

- Beautiful: Singing River (Ch 7)  
- Terrifying: Silent Grove (Ch 10)  
- Impossible: Upside-down waterfall (Ch 11)

**Bonus wonder, outside the locked triad (Ch 18):** the Understars' doubled false sky (Mirror Deep). The triad itself stays closed at three — this is an extra, not a fourth entry.

### The Understars (new floor, Ch 16–21)

A full **new floor with its own ecology** — not a reskin of forest / glass / root — reached by descending past the Bellows Hall. A vast, lightless-should-be cavern whose ceiling holds a colony of bioluminescent **driftlight moths**, fixed in slow-drifting clusters that read exactly like constellations; a still black pool (**Mirror Deep**) doubles the false sky underfoot. Its own nocturnal food web runs on borrowed starlight instead of sun: driftlights → glimmer eels (clean the pool, keep the water head clear) → duskhawks (hunt the drift lines). The basin's light-cued cycles (moss flowering, deer breeding windows) run off this floor's rhythm as much as they run off water — quietly paying off Lumi's Ch 3 "early flowering" note as something more than seasonal.

**Why it matters (reveal, not dump):** this is where the book shows the dungeon is bigger and stranger than a single level, without an Architect confession or naming a Vol 2 villain. The Jester goes uncharacteristically still here — "That's new." — then deflects with a joke before anyone can press. He doesn't recognize all of it. That's the tell: he "did not build the whole dungeon alone" (see `lore/architects.md`) — shown, never said.

**Function for the climax:** the Sluiceworks (the actual water valve for the cascade) sits at Mirror Deep's overflow. Fixing the cascade requires working *this floor specifically* — redirecting the wheel and re-seeding the pool with fallen driftlights to keep the light-cycle from collapsing alongside the water fix. Full detail: `lore/ecology/glass-garden-rooms.md`.

### Dungeon structure, Ch 16–21

Glass Garden stops being one room and becomes a **hub**: a floor above (Rafters) and floors below (Undercroft → Bellows Hall → **the Understars**, a full new floor with its own ecology → the Throat) open off it — each a distinct chamber, not a repeat of the surface biome loop, climaxing in a floor that proves the basin holds more than anyone mapped. Full room list, floor relations, and what's entered vs reserved: `lore/ecology/glass-garden-rooms.md`.

## Volume 2+ (seeds only)

- Extinct-predator question may connect to Stag / wolves / something returning.
- Root-creature whereabouts stay open.
- Do not resolve Architect before Vol 5.
- Do not resolve cosmic creation/consciousness.
- **Someone is corrupting the Jester's creation** (Vol 2 spine). Villain intro at end of Vol 2 or sooner — **do not introduce or name that figure in Vol 1**. The only permitted Vol 1 texture is the Low Gate (Ch 19, glimpsed/left inside the Understars) and Jester going quiet near it — deniable, no confirmation anything is "wrong" yet, just a colder door than the one he slept behind.
- **The Understars itself is a seed, not just the Low Gate.** "He did not build the whole dungeon alone" (`lore/architects.md`) is shown in Ch 18 via his stillness, never said. Vol 2+ can reveal more of what's down there without contradicting Vol 1 — nothing below the lantern-light was mapped.
- Stop expanding dungeon bible until Vol 2 planning.
