# Expedition Philosophies

Not everyone agrees with Niko. Every crew has a creed. Friction is ideological, not cartoon evil.

| Approach | Creed | Typical behavior |
|---|---|---|
| **Conquerors** | “We clear floors.” | Kill apexes, force routes, treat ecology as obstacle |
| **Scholars** | “We document.” | Maps, samples, journals; Niko’s nearest kin |
| **Harvesters** | “We only gather resources.” | In and out for goods; may overhunt keystones |
| **Pilgrims** | “The dungeon is sacred.” | Offerings, taboos, refuse “desecration” of nests |
| **Survivors** | “Go in. Come out. Nothing else matters.” | Minimal ambition; Marta sometimes sounds like this |

## Volume 1 contacts

- **Lampwick Company** — mostly Scholars with Harvester contracts (Niko’s old orbit)
- **Red Nail remnant talk** — Conqueror reputation (Marta’s wariness)
- **Ash Ribbon** (optional foil) — competent mid-depth; mixes Conqueror speed with Survivor caution

When philosophies clash on-page, someone should almost have a point.
