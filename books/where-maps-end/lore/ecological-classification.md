# Ecological Classification

Every creature belongs to at least one category. Bram and Niko think like biologists. Monster notes require a tag.

| Class | Role |
|---|---|
| **Keystone** | Ecosystem collapses or transforms without it |
| **Apex predator** | Top hunter; rare; shapes behavior below |
| **Opportunistic predator** | Hunts when easy; dangerous to the careless |
| **Scavenger** | Follows death and waste |
| **Pollinator** | Moves spores, mana-pollen, seeds |
| **Symbiote** | Mutual benefit with plant, beast, or place |
| **Parasite** | Lives on / in hosts; may drive behavior |
| **Decomposer** | Breaks down matter; soil and crystal rot |
| **Magical catalyst** | Enables or amplifies magical processes |
| **Ecosystem engineer** | Builds / redirects habitat (dams, tunnels, light) |

A species may be dual-tagged (e.g. apex + keystone). Combat should reflect the tag, not a loot table.
