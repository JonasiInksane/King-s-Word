# Ecology — Greenwood Basin (Volume 1)

A gigantic “almost understood” region, ~60–80 km across. Explored for ~1,800 years. Multiple ecosystems inside one named basin. People think it is well mapped. It isn’t.

**Volume title:** *Where the Maps Disagree*  
**Scientific question:** Why do the maps disagree?

## Wonder triad

- **Beautiful:** The **Singing River** — water flows normally; every stone makes music by current speed. Nobody knows why. No plot importance.
- **Terrifying:** The **Silent Grove** — birds, insects, wind: nothing. If it’s quiet, leave.
- **Impossible:** An **upside-down waterfall**. Unexplained. Distant lights beyond it = Vol 1 left-behind mystery.

## Settlements

### Mossgate (~3,000)

Expedition hub. Mapmakers, guides, cooks, guilds, monster markets.

**Map Wall:** Expeditions hang worn maps before leaving. Returned maps stay; unreturned fade. Centuries of layers. Notes, half-maps, one that only says *Don’t go east*.

### Old Lantern (~40)

Tiny outpost. People disappear. Cause open — do not solve in Vol 1.

### Root Camp

Temporary research station. Scholars. Lumi’s academy keeps a camp here.

## Hollow Stag

Twelve-foot deer. Tree branches growing from antlers. Size is secondary.

**Defining behavior:** It never breaks branches. **The forest quietly moves out of its way** — young trees bend aside as it walks. Readers should feel the parting long before they see the animal.

Almost never attacks — until migration pressure / territory collapse. Signs constantly: bent saplings in a path, silence at edges, deer trails that stop where trees lean wrong.

## Root-creatures — missing, not extinct

Gigantic underground organisms (earthworm-scale) that reshaped valleys for centuries. Geography “breathed.”

- None seen in decades. People assume extinction.
- Their local absence destabilizes the basin (Stag leaves, wolves migrate, moss fails).
- **Niko does not prove they live.** He notices evidence others dismiss: fresh roots, different age rings, recently shifted rock, an expedition log, a tunnel too smooth.
- End line: “I don't think they're extinct.” → “Then where are they?”

Do not confirm death. Do not close where they went.

## Regions and trails

### Trade Road

Merchant path from Mossgate. Feels like a normal dungeon approach. Waystations, chalk marks, familiar Expedition Rules. First uncanny: a Guild mile-marker stands in a dry ditch that 1987 maps call a riverbank.

**Camps:** Mile-Three lean-tos; official Guild rest circle.

### Hunter Forest

Dense woods. Crystal moss patches. Deer trails. Wolf scratch. Stag parting-signs begin here.

**Trails:** Salt Cut (harvester), Pilgrim Coil (longer, safer manners), Red Scar (burned Conqueror path — unstable).

**Camps:** Moss-ring clearing (avoid blue-glow at night); Root Camp spur east.

### Ancient Quarry

Cut stone, abandoned winches, watchtower stubs. **Too-smooth tunnels** under the cut — not tool marks. Fresh root fibers in one wall (dismissed as “old growth”).

**Camps:** Quarry shade; don’t sleep in smooth tunnels.

### Great Lake

Shoreline disagrees with all three dated maps depending where you stand. Ferry culture. Fishers swear the beach “walked” in their grandparents’ stories (treated as joke).

**Camps:** Ferry dock; east spit (don’t go east — Map Wall echo; leave unexplored if it tempts).

### Crystal Caverns

Moss gardens, deer browse, beetle clicks. Connects toward Singing River outlet.

**Camps:** Dry shelf above damp; never on thick blue moss.

### Collapsed Fortress

Ruin on a ridge. Old survey nails. Silent Grove in the hollow below. Upside-down waterfall in a cleft beyond the ridge — **distant lights past the fall: leave them.**

**Camps:** Inner courtyard only; not the Grove.

### Glass Garden (in-world name)

Overgrown glass domes. Trees through glass. Ancient irrigation. Living ecosystems still running. Feels like a weird ruin, not an institution.

Writer-only later name: **First Conservatory**. Never on-page in Vol 1.

Sealed chamber inside: sleeping ridiculous man (Jester). Late volume only.

**From Ch 16 the Garden becomes a hub** — a floor opens above (Rafters) and floors open below (Undercroft → Bellows Hall → **the Understars**, a full new floor with its own light/dark ecology → the Throat toward Mossgate). Full room-by-room breakdown, floor relations, and entered-vs-reserved status: `lore/ecology/glass-garden-rooms.md`.

4. Quarry tunnel not on any chart.

## Expedition Rules (local)

- If the grove goes silent, leave.
- Don’t camp on blue-glow moss.
- Don’t follow parting trees hoping for a shortcut.
- Hang your map on the Wall — or don’t expect a funeral song.
- Don’t go east (Wall saying; unexplained — respect the leave-behind).

## Natural Law notes

Mana vs water still holds at the lake. No hard law breaks required in Vol 1 (rumor only).

## If keystones shift (climax pressure)

Root-creature absence in the working layer → Hollow Stag leaves territory → forest stops “knowing” where to part → Glass Wolves push toward Mossgate roads → Crystal Moss patches bleach → beetle bloom → cascade. Party **prevents catastrophe**; does not “win” by killing the Stag.

Whatever the party does to redirect wolves, spare/guide the Stag, or stabilize a moss shelf — log in `tracking/ecology-consequences.md`.

## Left-behind mystery (Vol 1)

**Distant lights beyond the upside-down waterfall.** Seen. Not approached. Readers should want to return.

**Second, smaller leave-behind (new, Ch 19):** the Low Gate, at the far wall of the Understars beneath the Glass Garden. Colder and newer-feeling than Jester's own seal; roots grown the wrong way into the frame. Glimpsed only, not entered. No name, no villain on-page — see `lore/ecology/glass-garden-rooms.md` and `lore/plot-outline.md` (Volume 2+ seeds).

**Third leave-behind, structural (new, Ch 18–19):** the Understars itself — a cavern too large to map in one visit. The party works one corner (Mirror Deep, the Sluiceworks); the rest stays past their lantern-light. Proof the basin holds more floors than anyone charted, without answering what.
