# Ecology — The Mirror Veins (Volume 1 primary biome)

A near-continent of crystal galleries, black-glass rivers, and hanging villages. Guild maps disagree on how its seams connect. One biome can carry the whole volume.

## Scientific / volume question

**Why do the maps disagree?**

Working answer for Vol 1 (partial): routes changed — crystal growth and water-cuts rewrote paths. Opens: *Why did they change? Who remembers the old routes?*

## Wonder triad

- **Beautiful:** An underground aurora refracted through crystal lakes — colors crawl across the ceiling like slow lightning.
- **Terrifying:** Absolute silence. When the galleries go quiet, the Glass-Antler Stalker is already near.
- **Impossible:** A waterfall that flows **upward** into a ceiling vent, feeding a river on a higher ledge.

## Geography

- **Climate:** Cool, humid; breath fogs; mana-static makes hair lift near bloom patches.
- **Terrain:** Faceted crystal canyons, mirror-slick bridges, gravel beaches of black glass, vertical shafts.
- **Water:** Black-glass rivers (mirror surface, clear depth). Upward waterfall at the Vein Heart. Running water blocks casual mana crossing (Natural Law).
- **Seasons / light cycle:** Soft “day” when aurora intensifies (~18 hours); dim “night” when only biolume moss glows. Local “sunrise” = aurora swell.

## Flora

- **Dominant plants:** Mirrorfern (silver undersides); crystal moss (green normal / blue-glow stressed); lantern lichen.
- **Symbiosis:** Lantern lichen feeds pollinator moths; moths spread fern spores.
- **Medicinal use:** Green crystal moss poultice slows bleeding; blue-glow moss causes vivid nightmares if burned as incense.
- **Dangerous species:** Blue-glow moss thickets — camping here attracts scavenger swarms after “night.”

## Fauna

- **Glass Grazers** (herbivore / ecosystem engineer) — crystal-antlered deer-analogues; scrape salt from walls; their trails become paths.
- **Shard Pike** (opportunistic predator) — river fish with glass scales; strike boots in shallows.
- **Vein Moths** (pollinator / magical catalyst) — carry faint mana-pollen; Lumi’s samples.
- **Echo Crabs** (scavenger / decomposer) — clean kills; click when oxygen drops.
- **Glass-Antler Stalker** (apex / keystone) — silent hunter; antlers refract light to blind; ancient individuals may hesitate near the Jester (Vol 1: weird pause only — do not explain). New juveniles do not hesitate.
- **Bridge Weaver** (ecosystem engineer / symbiote) — silk spanning shafts; pilgrims treat weavers as sacred.

## Civilization

- **Who lives here:** Hanging villages of Veinfolk; seasonal Lampwick survey camps; pilgrim shrines at the upward fall.
- **Trade:** Crystal salt, moth silk, grazer jerky, map fragments.
- **Myths:** “The waterfall remembers who first made it climb.” “Silent galleries are funerals that haven’t started.”
- **Resources:** Salt, stable crystal timber, moth-mana vials.

## Ecology

- **Food chain:** Moss/fern → Grazers & moths → Shard Pike / Stalker → Echo Crabs.
- **Migration:** Grazers shift ledges when aurora weakens; Stalkers follow.
- **Competition:** Villages vs Harvesters for salt shelves; Weavers vs Conquerors who burn silk bridges.
- **If Stalker (apex/keystone) disappears:** Grazers explode → fern collapse → moth die-off → mana-pollen crash → villages lose light economy → opportunistic pike and crabs surge on weak parties. **Do not kill casually.**

## Expedition Rules local to this floor

- Don’t whistle in the crystal galleries.
- Don’t camp on blue-glow moss.
- If the upward waterfall slows, move — silence follows.
- Never cross the same mirror-river twice in one march without a ferry charm.
- Maps that agree are suspicious.

## Natural Law notes / known stresses

- Mana vs running water holds — for now.
- Rumors of a slime in a lower spur digesting a tin buckle (Biological stress — plant only; Vol 3 territory).

## Party interaction risks (permanent change)

- Killing an adult Stalker → grazer bloom cascade (track consequences).
- Burning Weaver bridges → migration redirects onto village paths.
- Abandoning camp on blue moss → nest for Echo Crabs next season (Dungeon Remembers).
- Overharvesting moths → Lumi’s samples cost the Vein light.
