# Natural Laws

Immutable principles — as far as anyone knows. When these break, the mystery is **physics / world**, not bad camping.

Tag violations by tier in tracking.

## Magical

- Mana cannot cross running water (must bridge, ferry, or spend to jump).
- Fire magic consumes oxygen (sealed caves punish fireball spam).
- Overdrawn mana scars local ecology (plants bleach, insects flee).

## Spatial

- Floors cannot overlap.
- Teleportation cannot cross floor / seam boundaries.
- A place cannot be two distances from the same seam at once.

## Temporal

- Time flows equally in all documented biomes (no “week outside, year inside” in trusted charts).

## Biological

- Slimes cannot digest metal.
- Dead matter does not spontaneously reanimate without a catalyst species.

## Ecological (as law, not saying)

- Predators never hunt while crystal moss is in full Bloom (Sleeping / Bloom interaction).
- Keystone extinctions cascade within a season if unbuffered.

## Status

All above are **believed true** at series start. Vol 3’s scientific question centers on breaks. Do not invent a canon “why” for breaks in this bible — log observations only.
