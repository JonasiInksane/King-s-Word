# Magic

Magic is ecological. You do not create fire from nothing; you manipulate natural magical processes.

## Modern practice

Mages learn **recipes**: gather mana → compress → release. Efficient on paper; wasteful in the field. Textbooks lag behind living floors.

Examples of process-magic:

- Healing affects organisms (blood, tissue, infection)—not abstract HP.
- Ice manipulates crystal growth and heat sinks.
- Light wards borrow from bioluminescent symbiotes or crystal refraction.

**Overuse changes ecosystems.** Bleached moss, panicked pollinators, oxygen debt in sealed caves.

## Principles vs recipes

- Modern casters: recipes.
- The Jester (secret): principles — “chemistry,” not bigger mana.
- He does **not** have more mana than anyone else.
- He does **not** teach or lecture. Offhand observations only (“Ice grows inwards, you know…”); others chew on them. Revelations land chapters later.
- Efficiency jumps (e.g. a spell becoming several times stronger) come from reduced waste, not buffs.
- No on-demand mid-fight power-ups as habit.

## Local obedience

Spells bend to Natural Laws and local ecology. A fire recipe in low oxygen fails or kills the caster’s air. Mana across running water fails unless bridged.
