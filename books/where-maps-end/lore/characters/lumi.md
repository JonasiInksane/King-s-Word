# Lumi

## Role

Young witch. Book-smart, field-new.

## Unique solve

Magic research. Ancient texts. Controlled experiments on magical processes.

## Home arc

Believed knowledge lived in books. Learns the living world is the greatest textbook.

## Prior parties

- Academy / scholar track; first true deep expedition is Volume 1.
- **Root Camp:** her academy keeps a temporary research station in the Greenwood Basin — she has been there on paper duty before; this is the first time she leaves it properly.
- Carries textbooks the basin already outgrew.

## Wound

Humiliation when a perfect recipe fails in living air. Fear she is only clever on paper.

## Want

To understand *why* spells work — not only how the steps read.

## Secret

Annotated a banned margin note about “mana that refuses water” without reporting it to her tutors.

## Voice

Precise, argumentative with texts, kinder than she sounds. Lights up when a principle clicks. Chews on Jester’s offhand asides later; may deny learning from him.
