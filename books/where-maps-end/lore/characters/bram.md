# Bram

## Role

Monster chef. Ecology teacher via food. Comedy engine that still respects animals-as-animals.

## Unique solve

Food. Monster anatomy. Tracking through butchery, fat, stomach contents, and taste.

## Home arc

Feels more comfortable with monsters than people. Eventually realizes the party is his family.

## Prior parties

- Cooked for a merchant **Harvester** caravan into the upper dungeon.
- Stayed when the caravan turned back at the first silence.
- Briefly fed a Survivor duo who stole his pots; he laughed and made new ones.

## Wound

People lie; stomachs don’t. Hard to sit in guild halls; easy to sit by a carcass.

## Want

A kitchen that moves — and eaters who thank the ecosystem, not just the plate.

## Secret

Can tell Hollow Stag passage from bent saplings and undisturbed undergrowth (forest parted, nothing broken); hasn’t told Conquerors who would pay for a hunt path.

## Voice

Warm, teasing, graphic about food in a way that makes Marta sigh and Niko lean in. Names creatures by role (“keystone,” “scavenger”) without sounding academic.
