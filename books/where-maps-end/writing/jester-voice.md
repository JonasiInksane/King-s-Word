# Jester voice

## Distilled success rule

If the Jester makes the reader laugh and then think five chapters later, the scene succeeded.

## Always

- Smiling, joking, relaxed
- Ridiculous clothes, bells, stick
- Childish and wise without announcing wisdom
- Shares only what he notices, remembers, or believes still true
- Observes (“Those trees are taller.”) — does not confess history
- **Offhand observations, not teaching / not lecturing.** Throwaway asides — half-joke, half-fact — that others may chew on later (“Ice grows inwards, you know…” / “Heat grows outwards, you know…”). He may wander off mid-thought or tell them to ignore him. Party learns from the line; he never frames it as a tip, never admits helping, never congratulates himself as a teacher. Lumi may deny learning from him.
- **Only cast member allowed to sound strangely quotable.** If Marta/Bram/Lumi already speak in polished metaphors, he loses uniqueness.
- **Not a damsel.** No Vol 1 plot about saving him; he’s the creator (sealed) and doesn’t need rescue.

## Never

- Cryptic wise man voice
- Cool mode / dramatic speeches / “I was holding back”
- Withholding for drama
- On-demand fight solves
- Early Architect confession
- Saying “First Conservatory” or naming himself as builder
- Explicit tips / instruction voice (“You’re carrying the heat like luggage. Let the air…”)
- Framing himself as teaching, tutoring, or correcting technique

## Vol 1 locked lines

**First line (wake):** "You're all terribly dressed."

**Final button:** Kneels at a flower → "I've never seen this one before." → touches it → quiet laugh → **"Good."** → "Let's go." Delighted the dungeon lived. No explanation.

## Example tones

"Those trees are taller."  
He poked the bark.  
"Were they shorter?"  
"Much."  
Niko waited.  
The Jester shrugged and kept walking.  
"I don't know."

"Can we eat it?"  
He held up a leaf.  
"You can eat almost anything once."  
Bram reached for it.  
"Twice is harder."

"Ice grows inwards, you know…"  
He was already looking at something else.  
"Ignore me."

**The Understars (Ch 18) — the stillness beat.** He goes quiet first, mid-step, looking up at the false sky longer than a joke would take. "That's new." Beat. Then, before Niko can ask what he means: "Or I'm still asleep. That would also explain the coat." Keeps walking. Never confirms which. This is the closest Vol 1 comes to admitting he doesn't know everything down here — land it as a flinch, not a speech. No second attempt to explain it that chapter.

At the Low Gate (Ch 19) — no line. He goes quiet instead, differently than the Understars stillness (that one was wonder; this one reads as caution). Silence is allowed to do the work a joke usually does.

## When wrong

Speechless or lightly embarrassed — "Well. That's embarrassing." — because the dungeon changed.
