# Dialogue guide — Where Maps End

## General

Everyone wants something. Push back. Understated. No anime screaming. No exposition speeches.

**If a line would make a great quote on social media, rewrite it.**

**Dialogue is the scene** when the beat is people: meeting, bonding, arguing, worrying, naming, comforting, teasing. Stay with the exchange. Hands may go idle. Do not choke every conversation with packing choreography.

**Activity is texture, not a leash.** Talk while packing is fine. Talk that *is* the packing scene’s whole point is better when relationship matters.

**Messy, not efficient.** Anime/LN dialogue breathes. Character beats between lines. Sometimes a whole page of talk with almost no plot job.

**Not every line characterizes.** Idle talk, “Shame,” nodding. No punchline required. Nobody needs the last word. Real talk often ends without resolution.

**Shared moments.** Early party needs one laugh (or quiet joy) that includes everyone—not just Bram-funny or Lumi-awkward. Coworkers → people who might become family.

**Not every line needs a reply** — but real conversations do exist. Prefer leaving a dangling “Good.” *or* letting someone actually answer when the moment wants warmth.

**Messy intros.** Don’t parade Meet A → Meet B → Meet C. Arrive mid-conversation; history already running.

Characters should not sound like writers. Simple but distinctive (not bland). **Never use `…` / `...` for pauses**—interrupt with action or the next line. See `style-guide.md` thinking speeds.

**Not first-person webnovel.** No self-aware joke tags. No system lectures as dialogue. Warmth through what they say to each other.

## By character

- **Niko:** Notices; **starts** conversations from curiosity he can’t let go (“Why’d you keep that one?” / “How long has that been up?”). Not only reactive questions. Rarely concludes. Softens theories. Quiet when noticing alone.
- **Marta:** Short; practical; always about reality. Philosophy from repeated *actions*, not memorable sayings. Can talk longer when logistics or the road is the topic — still plain.
- **Bram:** Food/care when it matters. Harmless flaws. **Not funny every line.** Sometimes “Morning.” and walks. Hit rate drops → funny moments land.
- **Lumi:** Starts explaining; stops; corrects herself. “Actually—” “No, wait.” Her talk scenes often *are* the scene.
- **Jester:** Only strange/quotable voice. Jokes first; observes; never explains. See `jester-voice.md`.

## Overlap

Groups are not A–B ping-pong. Three voices interrupt:

“Why’d you keep that one?” / “It smells wrong.” / “Leave him.” / “I’m answering.” / “I didn’t say stop.”

## Philosophies

Conqueror / Scholar / Harvester / Pilgrim / Survivor creeds color NPCs. Disagreement can be reasonable.
