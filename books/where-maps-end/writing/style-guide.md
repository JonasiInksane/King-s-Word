# Style guide — Where Maps End

## Voice

Clear light-novel readability. Concrete sensory detail (crystal light, wet stone, stew fat, silence). Short–medium paragraphs. No purple overexplain.

## Tone pulse

Cozy camp → wonder → work of mapping/cooking/magic → sudden prey-terror → aftermath that remembers the signs were there.

## What to avoid

- Boss-room video-game fights
- Jester cool mode or exposition dumps
- Niko sudden inspiration / secret lore dumps
- Cartoon evil guildmasters as the spine
- Closing cosmic mysteries early
- Misery tourism without leisure and beauty

## Home arc

Campfires brush belonging without speeches. Show through food shared, rope counted, jokes that land, maps redrawn together.

## Magic on the page

Show process and waste. Let environment punish bad recipes. Let Jester jokes plant Lumi payoffs chapters later.

## Names and culture

Guild songs, map auctions, shrines, crystal kids — sprinkle, don’t dump.
