## Thematic spine
**"What if love spoke in commands?"** The King's Word literalizes a real fear: that the people who most want to protect us could destroy us with a careless sentence. Haruki loves softly and survives; Kaito loves ferociously and is consumed. The war outside is a mirror of the war inside a friendship.

- Ch 1–3 = Haruki tells **Sugi** the Before (kitchen open → story-style past → kitchen close).

## The corruption map (Kaito's fall — mechanical, not moral)

Grounded in the **Backwash (反響)** from `magic-kings-word.md`.

```
STAGE 0 — Warmth       (Ch 1): loyal, funny, hot-headed. Word unknowing.
STAGE 1 — Guilt        (Ch 2, post-rooftop): catastrophic command. Ruined voice.
STAGE 1.5 — Permission (Ch 3): Mira reframes guilt. "Say that again."
STAGE 2 — Discovery    (Ch 5–6): realizes the words did it; darker thought — it could have SAVED her.
STAGE 3 — Use          (Ch 6–7): commands to control a world that took Ryn.
STAGE 4 — Dependence   (Vol 2–3): can't NOT command. Voice flattens.
STAGE 5 — The Hollow   (Vol 4 climate → endgame): the Word wears him; Vol 4 opens the door wide, does not finish the possession.
```

Rule for writing Kaito: **never a villain who chooses power — always a griever who can't put the gun down.**

### The two engines of the fall
1. **Backwash** — mechanical cost; empathy erodes.
2. **Mira / Remnant** — human permission; converts guilt into mission.

### Mira's faction thread
Anti-FDC cell → Amatsu Remnant convergence. She delivers a weapon and calls it salvation.
