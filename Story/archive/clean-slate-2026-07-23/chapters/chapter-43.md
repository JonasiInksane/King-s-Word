# Chapter 43 — Name the Girl
*POV: Kaito*

The elder does not do second cups for the same verdict.

She does third cups when the verdict needs a civil name attached — Serizawa Mei, east wing special, failed number, living flag, last recognized thread while the Word walks in commoner throats. Kurohata's incense pretends it is older than smog. Suzumori stands with shoes neat and humming offline. Mira sits equation-bright. I sit because living minds listen when the Word is not the only grammar in the room, and because the elder's hands still smell faintly of smoke-debt from a fire that made Mira useful.

"The girl is confirmed," the elder says. Not question. Inventory completed. "Civil heir. Claim. Not volunteer. Not chyron. Not Remnant invention for harvest comfort." Her eyes cut to Suzumori, then Mira, then me. "Split inheritance paid on paper today. Two spoons. One flag. You are the loud spoon. The soft one breathes in soot with her. Iori breathes beside her on Clear Day. You breathe at dusk."

Suzumori's breath sharpens. Small. Structural. "Confirming a child is not confirming a season."

"Confirming a child is how seasons stop being erased by Chinmoku ledgers," Mira says, mild, not looking at her. "Mei is reach. Reach requires naming."

"Reach requires living pulse first," Suzumori answers. "You keep putting the flag before the girl. Harvest language. Keeper law hears it."

The elder sets a cup down like a verdict without raising her voice. "Kanda Iori is already on the smog road. Soft contact. Honest day. He does not muzzle. He spends a day being a person — for this girl." Her eyes find mine. "You collect at dusk of Clear Day. Moving out. No pad speeches. No soft reunions. Legitimacy prefers living flags. Living flags prefer honesty until honesty becomes theft."

I taste metal. Schedule sits behind my teeth like furniture STAGE 5 already assembled — anger, corridor, reshape, *collect*, *king* practiced without correction because correction is unfinished. Iori's warm face flashes from the shed edge — funny mouth, pain under warmth, *I can be a person*, *next time enemies* still ahead on the calendar but already written in the boy's grin. He is moving into Susuichi while we speak. Clear Day is tomorrow. Dusk is the clause.

"Clear," I say. Finished enough.

Suzumori looks at me the way keepers look at bells that still ring true and might still be hung wrong. "Collect is theft with manners. The elder prefers theft. I prefer neither. I will witness."

"Witness," I repeat.

"Hollow pressure," she says, and the naming lands like a kept thing turned over in light — not accusation, not blessing, keeper vocabulary for what sits behind my teeth when STAGE 5 furnishes anger without asking. "The Word wears you. You wear the schedule. Mira wears the equation. I name the hollow so you cannot pretend it is only anger. Anger is human. Hollow is weather that stopped asking if humans get a season."

Mira's shoulder brushes mine — alignment, not comfort. "File keeper poetry later. Practice now."

---

Smog alleys do not forgive grammar.

Remnant cover rents a warehouse throat two seams from Auntie Bazzu's debt board — close enough to smell wet cardboard, far enough to deny flags. Afternoon before Clear Day: Mira runs intent drills without calling them STAGE 5, because naming is somehow still Haru's job and I am not ready to admit the hollow has a census.

"Kill the pulse behind the tarp," she says.

A runner — volunteer, not gray-green, Susuichi hire — stands where paper law would call it murder and code law calls it debt paid. Living mind. My mind only. The Word purrs. Intent steps closer than grammar. I say, "Die," and the clause finishes clean — not sleep, not mercy, not Habu's soft inventory — and the runner's pulse stops like a stamp completing.

The alley learns blood dialect without asking. Echo paints under my nose. I wipe it. Maintenance, not manners.

"Again," Mira says.

Compel next. A scarf-boy who thinks he's choosing to freeze because the debt board told him to stand still for practice. "Kneel. Forget your name for ten breaths." He kneels. He forgets. His eyes go furniture. The hollow applauds without hands. Anger furnishes the applause. Anger is not the whole room anymore. Hollow is the room.

Suzumori watches from the alley mouth, not humming, hands folded like kept things. "Hollow pressure," she says again, quiet, each word a bell refused. "You compel a living mind in a town that swears not to sell kids — and the town's code watches. Code towns remember Voices even when paper forgets."

"Code towns also rent us cover," Mira answers. "Practice is not parade. Practice is tomorrow's dusk."

"Tomorrow's dusk is a girl who laughed on Clear Day," Suzumori says. "Keeper law hears that sentence and does not hum."

I hear it too. The sentence sits in my chest like a bruise I scheduled myself — Iori spending honesty, Mei spending joy, me spending dusk. The elder's harvest. Mira's equation. My mouth. The soft spoon somewhere in tarp rooms, sticky, unowned, still filed as panic by a boy who roared my confession and stopped my pad with *Stop!* — twin metal, twin breath, split inheritance the elder confirmed today with a third cup and a civil name.

"Again," I say, before Mira asks.

Anger wants the alley to become Shizuhara's gallery — gray-green, Habu glass, Yano's cheerful disease. I do not give anger the whole building. I give it one pulse, one compel, one kill finished without sleep endings because sleep is mercy and mercy died on Level Two with vents. STAGE 5's tooth bites grammar lazy on purpose. *Stop breathing* almost lands as cleanly as *stop breathing now* — intent shaving conjunctions like fat. That should scare me. The scare arrives late and small and gets filed under progress by Mira's eyes.

"Cleaner," Mira says.

"Hollower," Suzumori says at the mouth, and does not look away when I meet her gaze.

"Hollow is not cleaner," I answer.

"No," she agrees. "That is why I walk."

---

Evening equation in a tarp room that smells like machine oil borrowed from Kurohata runners.

Mira writes no map — maps are for smog roads. She writes columns in air, voice mild as filing:

**Iori = soft contact / honest day / no muzzle**
**Mei = civil heir / flag / laugh if permitted**
**Kaito = collect / dusk / moving out**
**Haruki = soft spoon / turn or break / Chinmoku second spoon if soft ending invented**
**Sera = half-right / sky painted / hunt when cover slips**
**Clear Day = pretense / joy / countdown**

"Sera wrote in block letters," I say.

"Sera is half-right," Mira answers. "Half-right is worse than wrong. File her under countdown too."

"Suzumori names hollow pressure. You name variables. The elder names harvest. I name—"

"Schedule," Mira says. Not cruel. Accurate. "You name schedule until schedule names you back. STAGE 5 is not a door anymore. It is climate. Clear Day is weather inside climate. Do not soften collect into reunion. Reunion is unfinished. Unfinished is how we kill what we meant to save."

The reshape still rings in feeds — *I'd reshape this whole world* — cut into villain chyron while Habu's zero stayed soft. Silence came because I painted the sky. Sera's slate said so. Mira agreed. The equation does not care about my ledger entry on children I did not vent. The sky cares. Chinmoku cares. Half-right cares.

Iori's thread pulls tight on the smog road — warm face, honest day, Mei who corrects change and smiles like numbers were always hers. I have not met the smile yet. Iori has. Clear Day will pay the smile on purpose. Dusk will spend it. Collect is the elder's verb. Theft with manners is Suzumori's. Schedule is mine.

"At dusk," I say, flat, "we're moving out."

"At dusk," Mira confirms, "you finish the clause. No pad. No *Clear the pad*. No soft boy freeze you can file as panic. He knows what you are now — chyron taught him. He may still not know what he is. That is not your mercy to give."

"And Mei?"

"Mei is claim," Mira says. "Living flag. Alive. Legitimacy prefers living. The elder prefers harvest. Suzumori prefers pulse. You prefer—"

"Schedule," I say, because honesty is the only door left that is not hollow.

Suzumori enters without humming — shoes neat, eyes old, bell absent tonight. "Clear Day tomorrow," she says. "I will not walk beside collect. I will walk beside witness. If the girl laughs, I will hear it. If you steal the laugh at dusk, I will remember the season that broke."

"Seasons break," Mira says.

"Honest breaks are still breaks," Suzumori answers. "Keeper law inventories them anyway."

She leaves. The tarp room learns her discomfort like weather learns pressure drop — structural, not sulk, keeper friction against harvest math, the third path gathering distance while I choose the hollow road by continuing.

---

Night before Clear Day is not rest.

It is alley aftertaste and Echo maintenance and Iori's route checked twice — clearer edge, bottle-cap games, debt board seams, warehouse gaps where coated figures might listen without greeting. Kuroda stays unpaid on-page tonight — a rumor at the edge of smog, not a meeting. Tomorrow is joy borrowed on purpose. Tomorrow dusk is mine.

I walk Susuichi's perimeter with Mira's runners, not parading, covering — Remnant debt dressed as smuggler swear, black flags denied at the gate, Chinmoku brushed once already on the truck rut, Sera's slate still in memory:

**SKY PAINTED WRONG**

The sky remembers anyway. My reshape did not vent children. My weather painted proof for hunters and feeds and mute captains who price Shizuhara dead as evidence. Split inheritance confirmed. Civil name confirmed. Soft spoon in soot. Warm runner spending day. Girl as flag and pulse both — elder math, keeper warning, equation columns aligned.

STAGE 5's tooth breaks skin on grammar in alleys where code watches. Kill. Compel. Anger. Hollow pressure named and still worn. Schedule set:

**Collect at dusk of Clear Day.**

I do not sleep toward Haru. I do not sleep toward Mei. Sleep endings are for volumes that still believe rest is justice. Clear Day is pretense and joy and countdown. Iori is already moving into the laugh. Mira is already moving into equation. Suzumori is already moving into witness without hum.

I stay in smog climate and practice the dusk clause behind my teeth — moving out, no soft reunion, legitimacy's theft with manners — while Susuichi breathes coal-sweet and bottle caps click at the clearer edge like a clock that refuses Habu's soft voice.

Weather remaining. Hollow remaining. Clear Day remaining. Dusk remaining. The girl has a civil name now. The collect has a schedule. The king road I did not ask to own walks forward without correcting *king*, because correction would be unfinished, and unfinished is how we kill what we meant to save — and the elder did not ask me to save. She asked me to collect.

I collect the schedule like STAGE 5 collects mouths.

The market prices silence. The Order prices sky. The keeper prices seasons. The equation prices tomorrow.

Dusk will balance the columns.
