# Chapter 22 — "Across the Market" (near-miss that isn't a chase)

Pays the Ch 22 compass in [lore/plot-outline.md](lore/plot-outline.md): Haruki sees Kaito across a night market; neither can move (crowd, patrol, Mira); humanizing, wordless; **no pursuit**.

## Setup (same stretch as Ch 21 — compress)

- One or two nights after Gorō was taken. Still no word. Minato thinner; empty stool still there; Machi open but harder.
- Boys hunting scrap / watching for any rumor of where "voluntary interviews" go — **no breakthrough**, no mansion address handed to them. Frustration, not investigation montage.
- Festival night market still running under the shortened evening — brightness under glass.

## Beats (~2,000–2,400 words)

1. **Open on the hunt that isn't a hunt:** Short beat — asking carefully (depot, a freight cousin, Machi) where they take old men for interviews. Answers are fog: "heritage office," "the hill," "don't ask." Riku reins Haruki in when guilt starts driving. Land: they're going to the night market because Machi needs oil/vegetables before curfew, and because standing still in the bleach room is worse.
2. **Night market under curfew:** Sensory — lantern spill, grilled smoke, crowd denser because the evening is shorter (everyone shopping at once). Patrol presence: gray-green at the ends of aisles, not a full sweep. Joy still real. Keep it alive.
3. **The sighting (the chapter's heart):** Across a stall gap / over a steam cloud — **Kaito**. Same jacket language, taller-in-the-shoulders wrong, face that Haruki would know in any city. For one held breath they look at each other. No shout. No Word. No run.
  - Why neither moves: crowd between them; a patrol turning the corner; **Mira** at Kaito's shoulder (stillness, hand or glance that means *not now*); maybe Kaito's hands are full with something ordinary (bags, an old woman half-hidden — or *not*, if Bellkeeper stay off Haruki's recognition). Prefer: Kaito mid-errand, Mira steering, both frozen by the same math Haruki is doing.
4. **Humanizing, wordless:** What Haruki sees is not a monster — a boy who looks tired, thinner, still *him*. Maybe a half-step forward that dies. Maybe Kaito's mouth shapes something that isn't a command (a name, an apology shape) and doesn't finish. The market noise covers everything. This is the friendship still existing as a fact the plot hasn't killed.
5. **Break and after:** A cart / patrol / surge of shoppers cuts the line of sight. When it clears, Kaito and Mira are gone — not fled in panic, *extracted* by Mira's competence. Haruki starts after once; Riku's hand on his collar/shoulder: "Not here. Not with this many eyes. You want Gorō or a spectacle?" Haruki stops. Cost lands: he chose the human job and the near-miss slipped; or he chose not to burn the market — either way, no chase.
6. **Close:** Walk home under dying lanterns. Haruki says almost nothing; one line that he was *there*, same city, close enough to touch and untouchable. Riku: then the hill still has our tram driver, and tomorrow we invent step two. Empty stool / Minato light as the last image — not "Kaito sleeping," not mansion lights again.

## Guardrails

- **No reunion dialogue.** No "why did you leave." No Word use on-page. No Mira monologue.
- **No chase.** The point is the almost — desire + impossibility.
- **Don't resolve Gorō.** Still missing; this chapter is emotional pressure, not progress toward the mansion (that waits for Ch 23).
- **Don't reveal Bellkeeper / Aihara** to Haruki. If Suzumori is nearby, she's a silhouette Haruki doesn't clock as important — or she isn't on-page at all (cleaner).
- Motif: fresh market texture; don't clone Ch 14 warehouse near-miss structure beat-for-beat (that was chase-adjacent; this is frozen); no slips.
