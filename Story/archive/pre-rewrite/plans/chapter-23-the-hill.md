# Chapter 23 — "The Hill" (enter for a human reason)

Pays the Ch 23 compass in [lore/plot-outline.md](lore/plot-outline.md): Haruki and Riku go into the **ruler's mansion** for a *human* reason — help Gorō, not intel. Sets the board for Ch 24's parallel collision (Kaito/Mira there for Aihara; Haruki already inside).

## How they get in (LOCKED for this plan — community, not clues)

**Machi's voucher.** A released detainee / freight cousin / quiet message through Minato: Gorō is at the heritage liaison office (the mansion), "cooperative," and can be **signed out** by an employer/character witness if someone brings paper and shows up before the next transfer inland. Machi writes a blunt letter (he's her regular; freight questions are nonsense; send him home). She will not go herself — the hill already has her contributions. She asks the boys. Human errand. Bureaucratic crack. No stolen map, no registry.

(If you'd rather they sneak a service/kitchen route instead of a front-door voucher, say so before write.)

## Setup

- Night after Ch 22 (or next evening) — compress. Market almost still in Haruki's eyes; Gorō still the job.
- Joy allowed in small doses at Minato before they leave (tofu couple, empty stool, Machi's flat mercy).
- Riku: exits, no speeches, sidearm only if the curriculum already earned it and they won't fire unless cornered — prefer **bodies and paper first**, gun as last weight in a pocket.

## Beats (~2,200–2,600 words)

1. **The message / the ask:** At Minato — word that Gorō can be collected with a witness signature. Machi's letter. Cardigan folded into a bag (the coat they wouldn't let him take). Haruki wants to run; Riku makes it a procedure. "We're picking up a tram driver, not invading a country."
2. **Approach the hill:** North pine road at shortened-evening edge or just after speakers end. Walled garden, koi pond glimpsed through gates, polite wrongness. Service/visitor entrance: tea-suit energy, clipboard, their letter almost laughed at then stamped because the machine likes paper more than people. First on-page interior of the mansion — heritage plaques, soft carpets, FDC in the corners like furniture.
3. **Find Gorō:** Soft detention / waiting room / garden annex — not a dungeon. He's intact, tired, furious in a gearbox way. Fact-toll joke if he can manage it. Tells them they shouldn't have come; takes the cardigan like it matters. Signing starts. Hope is real for one page.
4. **The friction:** Paper incomplete — another desk, another stamp, "wait in the corridor," a clerk who needs the liaison's initials. They're walked deeper into the house. Riku marks exits aloud under his breath. Haruki feels the kitchen-of-the-war thought without naming Aihara (he still doesn't know that name). Optional: glimpse of a mild civilian at a garden table / koi (Aihara camera seed) — Haruki doesn't clock rank; Riku's shapes-instinct twitches and files it.
5. **Close — deeper than planned:** Signing stalls. A door opens on a wing they weren't meant to see — raised voices, or a sudden lockdown light, or Mira's stillness at the far end of a hall for half a second (Haruki unsure). Gorō is with them but not cleared. They cannot simply walk out. Chapter ends on the threshold of the night going wrong: **inside the landlord's house, job unfinished, Ch 24's collision already in the walls.** No Aihara interrogation yet. No kill. No Word clash.

## Guardrails

- **Human reason only.** They are there for Gorō. They do not come hunting Aihara or Kaito.
- **No clue-chain.** No list, no "here's where the lieutenant sleeps." Geography learned by walking.
- **Gorō alive** and on-page; prefer him mobile with them by chapter end (or mid-extraction) so Ch 24 can still use him as human stakes without a corpse.
- **Kaito/Mira:** at most a half-second edge glimpse, or none — their full collision is Ch 24.
- **Haruki still Word-ignorant;** no slips required.
- Motif: don't remake Ch 14 ambush; mansion aesthetic = polite dread (koi, carpets, heritage), not warehouse diesel; don't end on empty stool (spent) — end on a corridor / garden door / "wait here."

## Hand-off to Ch 24

Ch 24 = same night continued or minutes later: Kaito/Mira reach Aihara; Haruki (hiding / waiting / lost in the wing) overhears interrogation fragments (school night, dragnet) and witnesses the command-kill; fails to act; carries it. Gorō/Riku placement decided on write so Haruki can be the witness without the whole party stage-center.
