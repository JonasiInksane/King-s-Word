# Chapter 25 — "Lift" (Volume 2 climax)

Pays the Ch 25 lock in [lore/plot-outline.md](lore/plot-outline.md): Extraction from the logistics **helipad** with the **Bellkeeper** aboard. Kaito Words Haruki to clear escape; Haruki **counters unwillingly**; city shakes; Mira realizes two Voices on lift-off. Haruki does not cleanly understand. Volume closes on rupture + sky.

## Setup (hours after Ch 24 — compress)

- Gorō delivered to Minato / Machi (brief, warm, not a victory lap). Haruki still can't say the kill cleanly; Riku reads enough to stay with him.
- City wrong after Aihara's death — more vans, stack floodlights, air traffic that doesn't belong. Rumor of a craft on the hospital-stack roof.
- Haruki's intercept reason: mixed — stop Kaito / understand / the almost from the market / the desk-truth — not a clean hero motive. Riku comes because the curriculum doesn't leave soft boys alone on roofs.

## Beats (~2,400–2,800 words)

1. **Approach the stack:** Night. Western skyline. Floodlights. They use what they know (depot routes, outer yard from the clean run) — not a heist map. Patrol heat. Feet and fear.
2. **The pad:** Craft warming. Mira. Kaito. **Suzumori** on-page at last as silhouette/calm old woman — hooded or not; she *hums*; does not flinch when the air goes wrong. Haruki may not learn her title; reader feels keeper.
3. **Confrontation (no long reunion):** Recognition paid in looks. Kaito tries to clear the pad the only way he trusts now — a command at Haruki (*stop / stay / get down / don't follow* — exact, Mira-clean). Not murder-first; utility. STAGE 4 flavor: he schedules the obstacle away.
4. **Unwilling counter:** Haruki's throat/body answers without ownership — soft strain meeting hard strain. He does not know he is a carrier; it feels like panic, refusal, the room agreeing with his terror. **City shakes** — windows, trams, a physical wrongness bigger than two boys. Nosebleeds. Both hit by Backwash weather.
5. **Mira realizes:** On the rising craft she clocks *two Voices*, not one weapon + a soft boy. Seed of later recalculation. She does not explain it to Haruki.
6. **Lift-off / close:** Craft goes. Bellkeeper calm in the noise. Kaito's face — still him, farther. Haruki on the pad with Riku, city ringing, unable to name what he did. Volume ends on rupture + sky + the unanswered whom-question still in his pocket. No clean power-reveal. No Kuroda cameo required.

## Guardrails

- **Haruki does not knowingly own the Word** after this chapter — unwilling only; files under accident/panic if he files at all.
- **Living minds only** — commands people on the pad, not the craft engines.
- **Suzumori:** presence + hum + calm; no Amatsu lecture.
- Joy: thin — maybe one Riku line that keeps them human on the climb.
- Motif: don't reheat Ch 3 rooftop beat-for-beat; this is a pad, a craft, a city-shake, a departure — different geometry than Ryn's fall.
