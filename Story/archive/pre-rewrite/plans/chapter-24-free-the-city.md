# Chapter 24 — "Free the City" (mansion night / darkest)

Pays the Ch 24 compass in [lore/plot-outline.md](lore/plot-outline.md): parallel collision. Kaito/Mira there for **Lt. Aihara**; Haruki already inside for Gorō; witnesses the interrogation (school night / dragnet fragments — dramatic irony); watches Kaito *free the city of him*; hides; **fails to act**; carries it. Kaito's first **premeditated** command-kill — STAGE 3→4 threshold.

Continues directly from [chapters/chapter-23.md](chapters/chapter-23.md): lockdown-amber, trio on the bench, interrogation weather in the walls.

## Placement (so Haruki can witness alone)

- **Riku** takes Gorō toward a viable exit when a gap opens (service stair / garden gate) — the human job still running. Haruki lags half a corridor (drawn by the voices / checking the brass-plate wing / separated by a closing door). Not abandonment — logistics under lockdown. They mean to regroup at the visitor gate.
- Haruki alone (or nearly) becomes the ear and eye for the Aihara scene. Riku/Gorō reappear after, or Haruki finds them mid-flight with the kill already in his body.

## Beats (~2,400–2,800 words)

1. **Same night, seconds later:** Lockdown thickens. Clerk gone. Riku picks an exit and moves Gorō. Haruki — one look back at the precise voices — is cut off or chooses two steps toward the sound before the house seals the choice for him. Short. Costly.
2. **The room (Haruki hidden):** Garden office / koi-facing study — the mild civilian from Ch 23, now clearly the one who sits while captains stand. **Aihara.** Mira present, still. Kaito standing with the tired market face and something colder scheduled behind it. Haruki in a side door / screen / balcony shadow — can hear, can see enough, cannot safely interrupt without killing Gorō's odds and his own.
3. **Interrogation (Word as tool):** Kaito commands speech with Mira-coached clarity — *tell the truth about the school night / why the alert timing / what you wanted from the boys*. Aihara talks — eventually almost *willingly*. Fragments Haruki needs: Kōgetsu Municipal High flagged before the attack; acquisition cell; J-Alert wrong on purpose; the boys as **assets**. Horror = Aihara approves of their existence. Living minds only; no object tricks.
4. **The kill (cold, not monstrous):** After the talk, Kaito's premeditated line — framed as justice / freeing the city — exact lethal command. Aihara obeys. Backwash visible (nosebleed / flat voice after). Mira does not flinch; may look satisfied or simply complete. This is STAGE 4's door opening: commanding as something he *scheduled*.
5. **Haruki fails to act:** He could shout, run in, spoil it — he doesn't. Frozen by recognition (market boy / rooftop boy / this), by Gorō math, by the Word weather, by the almost-righteousness of what he heard. The failure is the chapter's second knife.
6. **Break / extract:** Alarm real now. Mira moves Kaito. Haruki runs the other way — finds Riku+Gorō at the agreed gap, or they find him. No reunion dialogue with Kaito. Gorō gets out. Boys get out. The hill is wrong behind them. Close on Haruki carrying the kill and the school-night truth without being able to say either cleanly yet — tees Ch 25 helipad chase/intercept.

## Guardrails

- **Premeditated, not panic.** Not Ch 3 / Ch 9 energy — colder. Aihara talks first; the kill feels almost righteous. That's the trap.
- **Haruki does not stop it.** Complicity through inaction (arc seed paid hard).
- **No full Amatsu dump.** Birthmark / split inheritance stays with Suzumori for later; Aihara gives dragnet/school-night piece only.
- **Suzumori:** optional silhouette only if needed for Ch 25 continuity; prefer off-stage until pad.
- **Gorō lives** and exits. Riku does not lecture kotodama; he gets them out.
- Motif: polite dread → sudden exact violence; don't end on mansion lights as beauty — end on the walk down the pine road / Gorō's breathing / Haruki unable to speak.
