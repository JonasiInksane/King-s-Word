## Thematic spine

**"What if love spoke in commands?"** The King's Word literalizes a real fear: that the people who most want to protect us could destroy us with a careless sentence. Haruki loves softly and survives; Kaito loves ferociously and is consumed. The war outside mirrors the war inside a friendship.

Ryn dies. Radio: Word awake / cover ears. Escape west. First deep Backwash. Haruki withholds easy absolution.

The split grows later (unpaid absolution, Word, Mira), not at a refused rite. (Her convince-him / come-with-me pressure grows later, only as she *sees* what his mouth can do.)
